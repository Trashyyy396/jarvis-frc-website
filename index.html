System Overview

Who We Are

J.A.R.V.I.S is a first-year FIRST Robotics Competition team founded by the Wheelhouse Foundation in Coppell, Texas. We're building more than robots — we're building a program.

FRC gives teams six weeks to design, build, and program a full-sized robot, then compete on a global stage. We're showing up to win.

We're a community team — open to any student in the Coppell area, regardless of school or background. No experience required. Just drive.

Core Directives

What We Stand For

01 — Compete

Elite Competition

We train and build to win. FRC demands engineering excellence, strategic thinking, and relentless iteration. We take the field seriously from day one.

02 — Connect

Community Impact

Rooted in Coppell, backed by the Wheelhouse Foundation. We run outreach events, mentor younger students, and make sure robotics isn't just for the few.

03 — Create

Real-World Skills

CAD, programming, fabrication, project management, media — every subteam teaches skills that matter beyond the competition field.
